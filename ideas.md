# My top 3 ideas

Picked from a list of 10 (see [notes/all-ideas.md](notes/all-ideas.md)). Ranked by how often the problem happens, whether I can reach the owner this week, and whether it fits 10–12 hours a week.

## 1. QR print queue (start here)

- **Pitch:** Students scan a QR code, upload their file, choose copies and colour, pay by UPI and get a token, and the shop just presses Print.
- **The business:** Stationery and photocopy shops near GNDEC first, then near other colleges and coaching areas in Ludhiana.
- **How they handle it today:** Students log into their own WhatsApp on the shop PC to download files (slow, and people forget to log out), or send files to the owner's number, where they pile up as "IMG_2034" with no names. Others bring pen drives, which carry viruses. At peak times one person handles everything, and it stalls when the helper is absent.
- **Who I'll talk to this week:** The owner of the stationery shop I already use, after the morning and lunch rushes (around 3–4 pm). Also the owner of a second print shop near another college.

## 2. Dairy milk account with WhatsApp billing

- **Pitch:** The dairy records only changes ("+1 L", "skipped"); at month end every customer gets a bill with a UPI link on WhatsApp automatically.
- **The business:** The dairy I buy from, then other dairies and milk delivery people.
- **How they handle it today:** Daily litres are marked in a register or on a calendar, bills are added up by hand at month end, and there are arguments over how much milk was taken and slow payment collection.
- **Who I'll talk to this week:** The owner of my dairy, at a quiet time (not during the morning or evening milk rush).

## 3. Canteen pre-order and token screen

- **Pitch:** Order and pay from your phone through a QR code; the kitchen sees orders on a live screen and calls your token when the food is ready.
- **The business:** The canteen I eat at, then other college canteens and dhabas near campus.
- **How they handle it today:** Students shout orders at the counter, orders are kept in memory or on paper slips, cash and change slow things down, and lunch-hour queues get long and orders go wrong.
- **Who I'll talk to this week:** The canteen owner or manager, after the lunch rush.

## Backup idea: private live streams for weddings

If one of the three fails when I test it: give wedding photographers and studios a private, branded HLS live stream with a recording, so relatives abroad can watch. It uses my HLS/RTMP skills and could pay more per event, but I don't know anyone in this business yet.
