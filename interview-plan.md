# Interview plan

Goal for this week: understand each owner's problem and what it costs them, **without pitching anything**. One real conversation per business, with notes written down the same day.

## Rules (from my practice interviews)

1. **Visit when they're not busy.** Never during a rush. Ask "kab aaun jab thoda time ho?" (when can I come when you have a little time?) and come back then.
2. **Ask about the past, not the future.** "Last time X hua, kya hua?" (the last time X happened, what happened?) beats "would you use…?"
3. **One question at a time.** No "and also…"
4. **No leading words.** Don't say "annoying", "hectic" or "difficult". Let them describe it.
5. **Follow up on surprises.** When they mention something unexpected (a helper, a complaint, a repair bill), ask more about it before moving on.
6. **Ask for numbers.** Price per unit, customers per day, money lost. Don't explain why I'm asking.
7. **When they raise an objection, ask about the last time it happened** instead of arguing.
8. **Don't promise features in the first conversation.**
9. **End with a next step:** a date to come back, or someone else to talk to.

## Prepared answers (only if they ask)

- **"Kitne da?" (How much?)** "Pehla mahina free. Uske baad, jitna fayda dikhega uske hisaab se. Tusi dasso, kinna theek lagega?" (The first month is free. After that, based on the benefit you see. You tell me what feels fair.) Never say "not much".
- **"Tu gayab ho gaya toh?" (What if you disappear?)** "Main isi area vich rehnda haan, college ithe hai. Kuch atkeya toh aap purane tareeke te wapas ja sakde ho, koi nuksaan nahi." (I live in this area and my college is here. If anything breaks, you can go back to the old way and lose nothing.)
- **"Dujeyan nu vi vechega?" (Will you sell it to other shops too?)** "Is gali vich sirf tuhaade kol, pehle chhe mahine." (Only your shop on this street for the first six months.)

## 1. Stationery / print shop I already use

**When:** About 3–4 pm, after the lunch rush.
**What I want to learn:** how files reach the shop, what goes wrong, and what it costs.

1. Kal lunch time pe jo rush tha, shuru se end tak kya hua? *(Walk me through yesterday's lunch rush.)*
2. Bachhe file kaise bhejte hain? Mujhe dikha sakte ho? *(How do students send files? Can you show me?)*
3. Din mein lagbhag kitne print jobs aate hain? Exam time pe? *(Roughly how many print jobs a day? At exam time?)*
4. Ek page kitne ka? Colour kitne ka? *(Price per page, black-and-white and colour?)*
5. Last time galat file print hui, kya hua? Paisa kisne diya? *(The last time a wrong file was printed, what happened? Who paid?)*
6. Kabhi kisi ka WhatsApp computer pe khula reh gaya? Phir kya hua? *(Has anyone's WhatsApp ever been left open on the PC? What happened then?)*
7. Last time helper nahi aaya, kya hua? *(What happened the last time the helper didn't come?)*
8. Kitne bachhe line dekh ke wapas chale jaate hain? *(How many students see the queue and leave?)*
9. Computer pe virus ya repair ka kitna kharcha hua hai? *(How much have viruses or repairs cost you?)*
10. Pehle koi software try kiya hai? Kya hua? *(Have you tried any software before? What happened?)*
11. Dukaan mein naya kuch lagana ho toh decide kaun karta hai? *(Who decides when something new is brought into the shop?)*

## 2. A second print shop (near another college or coaching area)

**When:** Mid-afternoon.
**What I want to learn:** whether the same problems happen here, or whether shop 1 is unusual.

1. Bachhe file kaise bhejte hain? *(How do students send files?)*
2. Sabse zyada rush kab hota hai? Kal kaisa tha? *(When is the rush worst? How was it yesterday?)*
3. Rush time pe kitne log kaam karte hain? *(How many people work during the rush?)*
4. Last time koi jhagda hua print ko lekar, kis baat pe? *(The last time there was an argument over a print, what was it about?)*
5. Din mein kitna kaam print ka hota hai, aur kitna photocopy ya binding? *(How much of the day's work is printing, and how much is photocopying or binding?)*
6. Pehle koi software ya app try kiya hai? Kyun chhoda? *(Have you tried any software or app before? Why did you stop?)*

## 3. My dairy

**When:** Midday, between the morning and evening milk rush.
**What I want to learn:** how monthly accounts work and where money is lost.

1. Kitne customers ka monthly hisaab hai? *(How many customers are on monthly accounts?)*
2. Daily kaise likhte ho kisne kitna liya? Mujhe register dikha sakte ho? *(How do you record daily litres? Can you show me the register?)*
3. Pichhle mahine ka bill banane mein kitna time laga? *(How long did last month's billing take?)*
4. Last time kisi customer ne bill pe bahas ki, kya hua? *(The last time a customer argued over the bill, what happened?)*
5. Abhi kitne logon ka paisa pending hai? Kitne din se? *(How many customers have payments pending right now? For how long?)*
6. Paise kaise lete ho, cash ya UPI? Kaun aake deta hai, kaun nahi? *(How do you collect payment, cash or UPI? Who pays in person, who doesn't?)*
7. Koi app try kiya hai hisaab ke liye? Kya hua? *(Have you tried an app for accounts? What happened?)*

## 4. College canteen

**When:** After 3 pm, after the lunch rush.
**What I want to learn:** what happens at lunch and what it costs.

1. Kal lunch time pe kya hua, order lene se khaana dene tak? *(What happened at lunch yesterday, from taking orders to serving food?)*
2. Lunch ke ek ghante mein kitne order aate hain? *(How many orders in the lunch hour?)*
3. Last time order galat gaya, kya hua? *(The last time an order went wrong, what happened?)*
4. Kitne log line dekh ke wapas jaate hain ya bahar kha lete hain? *(How many people see the queue and leave, or eat elsewhere?)*
5. Cash aur UPI kitna-kitna? Khulle ki problem hoti hai? *(How much is cash and how much UPI? Is giving change a problem?)*
6. Khaana bach jaata hai ya khatam ho jaata hai? Kal kya hua? *(Does food get wasted or run out? What happened yesterday?)*
7. Canteen ka contract kaise chalta hai, koi nayi cheez lagani ho toh kisse poochna padta hai? *(How does the canteen contract work? Whose permission is needed to bring in something new?)*

## 5. Wedding photographer / studio (backup idea)

**When:** Weekday, off-season if possible.
**What I want to learn:** whether families ask for live streaming and what they get today.

1. Pichhli shaadi mein kya kya cover kiya? *(What did you cover at your last wedding?)*
2. Kabhi kisi family ne live dikhane ko bola, bahar wale rishtedaaron ke liye? Kya kiya? *(Has any family asked for a live stream for relatives abroad? What did you do?)*
3. Last time live stream kiya, kaise kiya? Kya problem aayi? *(The last time you did a live stream, how did you do it? What went wrong?)*
4. Uska alag paisa lete ho? Kitna? *(Do you charge extra for it? How much?)*
5. Saal mein kitni shaadiyan karte ho? Kitnon mein NRI family hoti hai? *(How many weddings a year? How many have family abroad?)*
6. Aur kaun log yeh kaam karte hain area mein? *(Who else does this work in the area?)*

## 6. Hostel mess / PG

**When:** After lunch service.
**What I want to learn:** how the menu is decided and what happens to complaints and wasted food.

1. Is hafte ka menu kaise decide hua? *(How was this week's menu decided?)*
2. Last time students ne khaane ki complaint ki, kya hua? *(The last time students complained about food, what happened?)*
3. Roz kitne log khaate hain? Kitna khaana bach jaata hai? *(How many people eat each day? How much food is left over?)*
4. Kal kitna bacha tha? Usme kitna nuksaan? *(How much was left over yesterday? How much money was lost?)*
5. Mess ka contract kiske paas hai? Badlaav kisse poochke hota hai? *(Who holds the mess contract? Whose permission is needed for changes?)*

## After each visit

Write down the same day:
- 3 things I learned that I didn't know before
- Any numbers they gave (price, volume, losses)
- What they're already doing about the problem
- The next step we agreed on
