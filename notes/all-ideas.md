# All 10 ideas and how I picked

## What shaped the ideas

- **Skills:** JavaScript/TypeScript, Python, C/C++, React, Node/Express, Socket.IO, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, JWT auth, REST APIs, Linux, nginx, HLS/RTMP streaming. Building the software is not the hard part.
- **Time:** 10–12 hours a week alongside college. The goal is a small, steady income.
- **Selling:** I can talk to shopkeepers, though I'm not very confident yet.
- **Owners:** comfortable with WhatsApp and UPI, but not much more. Products must need only one or two taps from the owner.
- **Places I go every week:** a stationery/print shop, the canteen, a karyana store and a dairy. The family medicine shop is rural and the owners avoid tech, so it's out for now.
- **Problems I've seen myself:** huge crowds for printouts; karyana records kept in registers (udhaar, stock, purchases, sales); a repetitive mess menu; lost-and-found buried in the common WhatsApp group.

## The 10 ideas

| # | Idea | Who buys it | The problem | How they handle it today |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | QR print queue | Print and stationery shops near colleges | Crowds, slow file transfer, privacy risk, dependence on one worker | Customers log into WhatsApp on the shop PC, or send files to the owner's number |
| 2 | Canteen pre-order and token screen | College canteens, dhabas | Lunch queues, wrong orders, handling cash | Shouted orders, paper slips |
| 3 | Dairy milk account with WhatsApp bill | Dairies, milk delivery people | Disputes over litres, slow collection | Register or calendar marks |
| 4 | Karyana WhatsApp order desk | Karyana stores | Orders lost in calls and chats | Phone calls, paper |
| 5 | Digital shop register (udhaar, stock) | Karyana stores | Flipping pages, forgotten dues | Registers. Khatabook and OkCredit are free, so this is hard to compete on. |
| 6 | Mess menu planner and feedback | Mess contractors, PG owners | Repetitive menu, waste, complaints | Fixed menu, verbal complaints |
| 7 | Campus lost-and-found board | College or student council | Posts get buried in group chats | WhatsApp group |
| 8 | Tuition fee and attendance reminders | Tuition centres | Awkward fee follow-ups | Registers, phone calls |
| 9 | PG rent and electricity splitter | PG owners | Sub-meter calculations, chasing rent | Notebook, calculator |
| 10 | Private wedding live streams | Wedding photographers and studios | Relatives abroad want to watch | Public phone live streams, or nothing |

## Plan

Build one at a time. This week: talk to the owners (no code). Build first whichever owner sounds most interested. Get one shop using it daily for a month, fix what breaks, then sell it to the next shop. Prices are guesses until the owners give real answers.
